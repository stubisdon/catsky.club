# Sugar Daddy — micro-influencer discovery & outreach

Research completed 2026-07-21. This is a **research and drafting file only**: nobody has been contacted, no money is authorized, and all links are public-facing submission or profile routes.

## Summary

I ran LA-local, indie/bedroom-pop, sad/heartbreak-playlist, and submission-route searches, then checked the resulting public pages for a live editorial/curation practice, an explicit contact route, LA relevance, and obvious mismatch with the song's intimate, non-exploitative framing. The broad sweep yielded **58 raw leads**; **12 passed** the first filter below; **6 are LA Tier 1**, **6 are US/non-LA Tier 2**, and **3 are paid-flagged** because their public page presents a paid placement. The rest were excluded as large/general outlets, artist-only accounts, stale/unclear properties, non-US targets, or no usable contact route. This is deliberately a smaller send-first list than the 15–20 target: it favors checked, reachable fits over invented social metrics.

The first five sends are:

1. **SD-01 Coconut Spaceship** — LA/SoCal, explicitly accepts unreleased local music and features.
2. **SD-02 Beatique / Liz O.** — LA DJ/journalist with an indie and alt-culture editorial voice.
3. **SD-03 the loop** — Silver Lake-rooted listener/editor with an explicit email route.
4. **SD-04 Rising Artists Blog** — LA route with a free submission option; paid option needs approval.
5. **SD-07 Donewaiting** — emerging-artist editorial route that explicitly includes LA artists.

### Important limits

- Social platforms do not expose dependable public follower, recent-post, or post-level engagement data through ordinary web results. Those fields are **unknown**, not estimated. Before sending a DM to a social-first account, manually check its most recent 6–10 posts, calculate `(likes + comments) / followers`, and reject below 4% or any fake-engagement pattern.
- A passing entry means the public site fits and has a live-looking route; it does **not** guarantee editorial interest, audience size, or a post.
- **Goal: traffic + subscribers.** The point of every send is to drive people to the YouTube premiere, the IG, and the personal site. Push the video confidently and always ask for a premiere-day share that links back — a tasteful placement with no link-back does not serve this goal. Prioritize accounts that can actually post a clip and tag/link, not just list a review on a low-traffic blog.
- The honest frame is an alternative-pop song/video about a complicated toxic relationship. Be honest and a little mysterious; never pitch it as recovery advice or use trauma as a hook, and never ask a mental-health/recovery creator to treat the song as testimony.

## ICP & method

The working ICP is a 2k–50k, US-facing curator, local scene editor, playlist owner, or careful adjacent-community creator with an intimate voice and a credible posting habit. LA is Tier 1. The art is the entry point and the traffic is the goal: an early look at a mysterious, honest alt-pop video, in exchange for a premiere-day share that sends their audience to the YouTube premiere, IG, and site. Favor accounts that can post a clip and link back over blogs that only publish a static review. Enforce the floor: do not promote a social prospect to `queued` without a manual follower/engagement check (2k–50k real, ≥4%).

### Scoring rule used here

The standard score is 0–5 for content fit ×3, engagement ×2, geo ×2, authenticity ×2, reliability ×1, and contactability ×1 (maximum 55). In this first pass, **unknown engagement is recorded as unknown and contributes 0 to the provisional score**—not because it is bad, but to avoid pretending we measured it. `authenticity: ok` means the official public site has a clear identity/contact route and no obvious pod/follower-scheme red flag; it is not a quantitative audience audit. Scores should be re-run after the operator checks the profile/feed.

## Operating guide

1. First open each social/profile link or editorial site. Add one true detail to the first sentence; confirm the account posted within 30 days and that its audience looks US/LA-relevant. For social accounts, calculate engagement across 6–10 posts and reject below 4% or any obvious pod/fake-follower pattern.
2. Send SD-01 through SD-04 in the first 48 hours. Share the private preview, trailer, stills, and 1–2 vertical clips only after interest; do not attach a giant press kit to the first contact. Send SD-07 through SD-09 the next day.
3. If someone says yes, reply: "yes! thank you 🐾 here's the private link [PREMIERE LINK] — it premieres [DATE/TIME PT] on [YT CHANNEL]. a share on premiere day (or the day around it) would help it the most, and a link back to the youtube premiere or [IG] is what actually sends people my way. do whatever feels natural for your audience." Log the date, format, link, whether it links back, and any disclosure requirement.

Offer gifting (early access or authorized free membership) only after a genuine yes. Offer paid placement only to SD-04 or SD-06 after the artist approves a ceiling and the outlet gives a current rate, deliverable, date, and credible audience data. Any paid or gifted post must use the platform’s paid-partnership disclosure and `#ad` where applicable. Never ask an adjacent recovery/mental-health creator to use the song as advice or testimony.

When a post goes live, record the URL, timestamp/PT, disclosure, content format, first-24-hour views/comments if visible, clicks to `[PREMIERE LINK]` if measurable, and whether it landed on premiere day ±1. Thank them once, then let the work breathe.
