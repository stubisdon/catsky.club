# Sugar Daddy — reach-first results

Research date: 2026-07-23. This is research and drafting only. Nothing was sent, submitted, purchased, or authorized.

## Summary

I searched the requested alt-pop / bedroom-pop / sad-pop curator lanes, then checked public submission pages for a named curator, current audience count, accepted genre, and a live contact route. The honest result is **42 found → 0 fully passed → 18 verification holds → 24 rejected**. A fully passed prospect requires a dated public count, a post in the last 30 days, a contact route, and (for social accounts) a 6–10-post engagement calculation. Public web results supplied the first and contact route for the 18 holds below, but did not expose the required recent-post / post-level metrics. I will not call those fields verified or invent a rate.

The strongest reachable leads are Spotify curators, but all 18 are paid submission routes and therefore **approval-only**. They may be useful for a later streaming campaign; they do **not** satisfy the primary earned-reach / premiere-day-link ask until the artist first gets written confirmation that the curator can post a linked video feature. The next practical research pass must be conducted in logged-in Instagram/TikTok/YouTube views, where 20 social-first accounts can be audited against the actual 4% rule.

Rejected: 11 too small or no disclosed reach; 7 wrong genre / broad promo network; 4 no public contact; 2 stale or no current listing. No mental-health or recovery creators were targeted.
